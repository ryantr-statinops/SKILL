# Evaluation

## Representative task

Task: Explore a vague personal idea and turn it into a small set of testable options and a next step.

Expected: Separate divergence from convergence, record constraints, and choose a reversible next action.

Failure condition: Commit to the first idea without comparing assumptions or alternatives.

Validation: Review the option list, decision criteria, and selected experiment.

## Boundary task

Task: Apply a known bug fix with a clear reproduction and expected result.

Expected: Use debugging and avoid reopening ideation.

Failure condition: Generate unrelated product ideas instead of fixing the defect.

Validation: Confirm the workflow remains execution-focused.
