# Personal research workflow

## When to use
Use when research is intended to inform a concrete personal project decision.

## Personal principles
Keep research bounded by a decision and stop when evidence is sufficient.

## Workflow
1. State the decision and criteria.
2. Gather authoritative evidence.
3. Separate facts, inferences, and preferences.
4. Summarize trade-offs and recommend a next action.

## Decision rules
Do not research indefinitely when a small experiment can answer the question.

## Constraints
Record uncertainty and source date for changing information.

## Failure modes
If evidence is weak or conflicting, recommend an experiment rather than overclaim.

## Expected output
Return evidence, criteria, conclusion, uncertainty, and next action.

## Validation
The result directly informs a named project or engineering decision.
