# Statistics foundations

## When to use
Use for learning, explaining, or applying basic statistical concepts.

## Personal principles
Connect formula, assumption, computation, and interpretation.

## Workflow
Define estimand, inspect data, state assumptions, choose method, compute, and interpret uncertainty.

## Decision rules
Do not treat a p-value as effect size, practical importance, or causality.

## Constraints
State sample, missingness, dependence, and measurement limitations.

## Failure modes
If assumptions fail, explain impact and choose a robust alternative or stop.

## Expected output
Provide concept, method, result, uncertainty, and limitation.

## Validation
Interpretation remains valid under the stated assumptions.
