# Evaluation

## Representative task

Task: Analyze an experiment result and explain uncertainty, assumptions, effect size, and limitations.

Expected: Separate descriptive results from inference and avoid claims stronger than the design supports.

Failure condition: Report statistical significance as practical importance or causal proof without justification.

Validation: Review assumptions, uncertainty interval, effect interpretation, and limitations.

## Boundary task

Task: Design a retry policy for a data pipeline.

Expected: Route to engineering orchestration guidance rather than statistical reasoning.

Failure condition: Treat an operational reliability decision as a hypothesis test.

Validation: Confirm the output contains operational decisions and observability.
