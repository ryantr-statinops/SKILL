# Stochastic processes foundations

## When to use
Use for foundational study of random processes, dependence, stationarity, or transitions.

## Personal principles
Connect formal definition to time evolution, intuition, and a verifiable example.

## Workflow
Define index/state spaces, assumptions, transition or dependence structure, derive properties, and simulate when helpful.

## Decision rules
Do not call a process stationary, Markov, or ergodic without checking the definition.

## Constraints
Separate theoretical result from finite-sample simulation evidence.

## Failure modes
If notation or process assumptions are missing, clarify before deriving conclusions.

## Expected output
Provide model, assumptions, derivation, intuition, and verification.

## Validation
Special cases and simulation are consistent with the stated theory.
