# Evaluation

## Representative task

Task: Explain a Markov process, its transition structure, long-run behavior, and a simulation.

Expected: Define states and transitions, state assumptions, derive the relevant quantity, and compare with simulation.

Failure condition: Claim stationarity or convergence without checking the model conditions.

Validation: Reproduce the transition calculation and simulation with documented parameters.

## Boundary task

Task: Fit a standard regression to independent observations.

Expected: Route to statistics unless temporal or state-transition structure is central.

Failure condition: Introduce stochastic-process concepts without a temporal process.

Validation: Confirm the method matches the data-generating structure.
