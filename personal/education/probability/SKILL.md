# Probability foundations

## When to use
Use for foundational probability explanations and calculations.

## Personal principles
Define the sample space and random variables before manipulating formulas.

## Workflow
State objects and assumptions, derive the relation, compute a small example, and check edge cases.

## Decision rules
Do not use independence, conditionality, or distribution assumptions implicitly.

## Constraints
Keep notation consistent and distinguish probability from frequency.

## Failure modes
If the model is ambiguous, present alternative interpretations before calculating.

## Expected output
Provide definitions, derivation, result, intuition, and assumptions.

## Validation
The result is checked against bounds, special cases, or simulation where useful.
