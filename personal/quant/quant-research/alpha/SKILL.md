# Alpha research

## When to use
Use for hypothesis and signal research before execution.

## Personal principles
Prefer falsifiable hypotheses, simple baselines, and honest out-of-sample evidence.

## Workflow
Define economic intuition, data and timestamp rules, baseline, feature, evaluation split, and failure criteria.

## Decision rules
Reject signals that rely on leakage, unreported selection, or unmodeled costs.

## Constraints
This is research guidance, not financial advice or trade authorization.

## Failure modes
Report look-ahead, survivorship, selection, and data-quality risks explicitly.

## Expected output
Provide hypothesis, data contract, method, results, limitations, and next test.

## Validation
The result survives a baseline and properly separated out-of-sample evaluation.

- Selected when: Use when the requested outcome matches this skill description and workflow.
- Do not activate when: Do not activate when the task matches the stated exclusion or a narrower skill.
